# Sales Performance Dashboard & Data Analysis | Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales data using Microsoft Excel to generate meaningful business insights. It includes a sales dashboard, KPI cards, regional and product-category analysis, a histogram, descriptive statistics, and regression analysis.

**Project Type:** Data Analytics / Business Intelligence  
**Tool:** Microsoft Excel  
**Dataset Size:** 200 records  
**Analysis Period:** April 2024 – April 2025

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Create KPI cards to summarize key business metrics.
- Compare sales across different regions and product categories.
- Analyze sales distribution using a histogram.
- Apply descriptive statistics to understand sales data.
- Perform regression analysis to explore the relationship between sales and profit.
- Present business insights through a visual dashboard.

## 📊 Dataset Description

The dataset contains the following main columns:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| Customer_ID | Customer identifier |
| Customer_Name | Customer name |
| Region | Sales region |
| Product_Category | Product category |
| Sales | Sales amount |
| Quantity | Quantity recorded |
| Discount | Discount applied |
| Order_Date | Transaction date |
| Profit | Profit amount |

**Regions:** Central, East, North, South, West

**Product Categories:** Books, Clothing, Electronics, Furniture, Office Supplies

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | 195,217.76 |
| Total Profit | 68,287.01 |
| Total Records | 200 |
| Average Discount | 9.725% |

*Note: Currency units are not specified in the source dataset. Total records represent dataset rows and not necessarily unique customers or verified orders.*

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 1. Regional Sales Analysis

| Region | Total Sales |
|---|---:|
| West | 44,083.58 |
| Central | 43,468.33 |
| South | 40,248.79 |
| North | 34,975.42 |
| East | 32,441.64 |

**Findings:**
- West has the highest total sales.
- Central is the second-highest region.
- East has the lowest total sales.

### 2. Product Category Analysis

| Product Category | Total Sales |
|---|---:|
| Books | 47,002.46 |
| Clothing | 41,927.59 |
| Electronics | 41,044.00 |
| Furniture | 36,612.89 |
| Office Supplies | 28,630.82 |

**Findings:**
- Books generate the highest total sales.
- Clothing and Electronics are the next-highest categories.
- Office Supplies generates the lowest total sales.

### 3. Profit Analysis

- Total recorded profit: 68,287.01.
- Central generates the highest total profit among the regions.
- Clothing generates the highest total profit among the product categories.

Sales and profit should be evaluated together to understand business performance more effectively.

## 📉 Data Analysis and Visualization

### Sales Dashboard
Provides an overview of key metrics using KPI cards and charts.

### Regional Sales Analysis
Compares total sales across five regions to identify high-performing and low-performing areas.

### Product Category Analysis
Highlights the contribution of each product category to total sales.

### Histogram — Distribution of Sales Amounts
Analyzes how sales amounts are distributed across different value ranges.

- **X-axis:** Sales Amount
- **Y-axis:** Number of Orders

The histogram helps identify common sales ranges and the spread of transaction values.

### Descriptive Statistics
Uses measures such as mean, median, standard deviation, variance, minimum, and maximum to summarize sales data.

### Regression Analysis
Explores the relationship between sales and profit using Excel's regression output.

The reported R-squared is approximately 0.598, indicating that the fitted model accounts for around 59.8% of the observed variation in profit in this sample. This does not establish a causal relationship.
